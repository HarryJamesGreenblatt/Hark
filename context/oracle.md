# 🔮 The Oracle — HARK's seeing, interpreting presence (identity & north star)

> **Status:** canonical identity doc. Supersedes the retired monikers **HAL**, **Cristóbal**, and
> **"crystal ball"**, which had fragmented one presence across three names. Written 2026-09-02.
>
> **One-liner:** *The Oracle is HARK's eye and mind — a sound-reactive presence that watches the
> conversation and renders a live, didactic **Vision** of what it means.*

## Why "the Oracle"

The name resolves a conflation. The same presence had been called **HAL** (the red, sound-reactive
eye — a 2001 aesthetic homage), **Cristóbal** (the interpreting "mind" — an engine codename), and the
**crystal ball** (the dilated full-window view). One thing, three names.

**Oracle** absorbs all three because the word already carries both halves of what this is:

- an **oracle** is a **seer** — it evokes the **eye**, the visual form HARK already wears; and
- an oracle is a **purveyor of visions** — it evokes the **didactic Vision** the presence renders.

It is **not predictive** (HARK reads what *is* being said, it does not foretell) — but the imagery of a
seer that "has a vision" is pervasive enough to fit, and it unifies the face and the mind under one name.

> **The red-eye aesthetic stays; only the name "HAL" is retired.** The metallic-framed, glowing-red,
> sound-reactive eye is unchanged — it's now simply **the Oracle's eye**.

## Anatomy

| Part | What it is | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **The Oracle's eye** | the metallic-framed red eye in the bar — dim when idle, pulsing with the captured audio (pupil dilates on **bass**, highlight drifts on **treble**). Click it to open Vision. | `Hark.App/OverlayWindow` (`OracleEye`, `OracleCornea`, `OracleGlow`, `OracleScale`; the large replica `OracleEyeBig`) |
| **The Oracle's mind** | the art-director judgment that distils the live conversation into a visual concept + a structured diagram concept | `Hark.Oracle` / `Hark.Oracle.Vision` (`ConceptDesigner`, `InfographicDesigner`) |
| **The Vision** | what the Oracle renders — a **dual layer**, conjured in parallel every beat: a **native WPF radial mind-map** behind the eye (the eye is its empty-centre hub) **+** a **FLUX cinematographic scene** inside the pupil | `Hark.Oracle.Vision` (`VisionRenderer`) → `Hark.App/OverlayWindow` (`VisionCanvas`) |

Code layering stays as it was — the Oracle is a sibling stack **on top of** the engine:
**`Hark.Core`** (the ear) → **`Hark.Oracle`** (the Oracle's mind) → **`Hark.Oracle.Vision`** (its Vision).

## How the Vision works (unchanged by the rename)

Clicking the Oracle's eye dilates it into a full-window **Vision** page (a corner→centre match-cut zoom;
the large eye stays audio-reactive). While open, the Oracle **autonomously re-conjures on topic beats**.
The key architectural turn (EP25): **a diagram is structured data — drawn natively, not generated by an
image model** — so the didactic mind-map is a native WPF vector layer (exact hub, crisp text, instant),
freeing the generative model (FLUX.2-pro on Foundry, provider-agnostic) for the pictorial pupil scene it's
actually good at. Every conjured beat is kept on a reviewable **timeline**, and the whole session (with the
Vision slideshow) exports via the multi-format **Keep** report.
